\noindent
\textbf{\emoji{party-popper} Skyzone Variant.}  
Follow the usual rules of Skyzone (or Skyzone Shape), but there’s no central discard pile.
\\
\\
\noindent
\textbf{Each player has their own discard pile.}
\\
\\
\textbf{When drawing,} you may choose either the central deck or any discard pile — yours or another player’s.
\\
\\
\textbf{When discarding,} place the card on your own discard pile.
}
