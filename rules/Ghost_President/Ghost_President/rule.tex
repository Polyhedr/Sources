\noindent
\textbf{\emoji{arrows-counterclockwise} Setup.}  
Deal the cards including a dummy player. Their cards are discarded. The player with the \textbf{yellow 10} starts.
\\
\\
\noindent
\textbf{\emoji{dart} Goal.}  
Be the first to get rid of all your cards.
\\
\\
\noindent
\textbf{\emoji{video-game} How to play.}  
On your turn, you can:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Play a set} of identical cards (singleton, pair, triple...).  
    If there's already a set on the table, you must play the same number of cards with a \textbf{higher or equal value}.
    
    \item \textbf{Pass.}  
    You can’t play again until the cycle ends.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
\emoji{pushpin} \textbf{Jokers} can replace any card in a set.
\\
\\
\noindent
\emoji{warning} The cycle ends if:
\begin{itemize}
    \item All other players pass.
    \item A set of 12s is played.
    \item A set matching the previous one (same value, at least 2 cards, no jokers) is played.
\end{itemize}
The last player to play starts the next cycle.
\\
\\
\emoji{pushpin} \textbf{You can’t end the game with a set of 12s} — if you do, you lose.
